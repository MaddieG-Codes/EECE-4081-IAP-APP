## 5. Overall Assessment

The AI was effective at identifying the major functions of the Southern City Explorer application, particularly city selection, location categories, searching, and navigation. However, the generated requirements were not sufficient to serve as the final requirements specification without human review. The AI missed some specific behaviors and acceptance conditions and also introduced features that were not part of the original project scope. The elicitation process demonstrated that AI can be useful for generating an initial requirements list, but the generated requirements must be reviewed against the actual project goals before being accepted.
